# E. Glossary

**Community block (Block B).** All eleven Block B measures: the four CDC Social Vulnerability Index themes, the Area Deprivation Index, the Index of Concentration at the Extremes for income, the uninsured share and the limited-English share; the two access measures, rurality (the USDA Rural-Urban Continuum Code) and primary-care physician density; and market concentration (the Herfindahl-Hirschman index of Medicare discharges). The variance decomposition's community share, and any model "with" or "without" Block B, refer to this block.

**Disadvantage group.** The ten community-block measures other than market concentration. Model class reliance on disadvantage, the disadvantage contrast (each measure moved from its more advantaged to its more disadvantaged tenth, with the income index and primary-care density reversed), and the per-measure reliance and total importance results all refer to this group. Market concentration is a property of the hospital's market, not of its patients' neighborhoods, so it is kept out of the group and stays in the model as a control.

**Model-implied disadvantage and community contribution.** A hospital's model-implied disadvantage (the payment simulation's ranking) is the fitted contribution of the disadvantage group to its predicted PSI-90. Its community contribution (the suppression check) is the fitted contribution of the whole community block, market concentration included.

**Material deprivation and demographic composition.** Two parts of the disadvantage group, used for the contrast's support. Material deprivation is ADI, the income index, SVI themes one and four and the uninsured share. Demographic composition is SVI themes two and three and the limited-English share. Rurality and primary-care density are reported apart from both.

**ADI (catchment).** The Area Deprivation Index as used here is a catchment average of published block-group national percentile ranks. The Neighborhood Atlas publishes ranks for block groups. Each tract's value is the population-weighted mean of its block groups' ranks, and each hospital's value is the discharge-weighted mean over its catchment's tracts (or counties, at county grain). It is a weighted average of ranks, not an ADI computed for the catchment, and it is labeled "catchment ADI (mean block-group rank)" wherever space allows.

**Structure block (Block A).** The eleven hospital characteristics in every model: beds, average daily census, occupancy, residents per bed, teaching status, case mix index, Medicare volume, disproportionate-share percentage, wage index, operating margin and uncompensated care as a share of net patient revenue. Ownership and system membership are not in it; the supplement reports a sensitivity analysis that adds them.

**Policy block (Block C).** Case-mix drift, contract-labor share and the two state tort-reform measures.
