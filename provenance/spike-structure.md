### Fiscal-year alignment of the Impact File

plus one quarter past it.

The FY 2025 Impact File is built on FY 2023 MedPAR (2022-10-01 to 2023-09-30), which

and volume fields, and FY 2026 for the payment-policy fields.** The FY 2025 vintage
is the better-centred measure of the case mix that produced the observed PSI rates.
Record this as a specification-space axis rather than an unexamined default: the
Phase 3 enumeration should include "Impact File vintage: FY2025 vs FY2026."

## 4. Block C: HCRIS Worksheet S-3 contract and agency labor — **the population rate is 90.7%**

This was the specific question Task 0.3 was set to answer with a number.

The Cost Report PUF exposes the S-3 contract labor line directly as
**`Contract Labor: Direct Patient Care`**, alongside **`Total Salaries (adjusted)`**.
No raw HCRIS worksheet extraction is required.

### Population rate

| Measure | Result |
|---|---|
| Frame hospitals with an FY 2023 cost report | 2,798 (99.9% of 2,801) |
| `Contract Labor: Direct Patient Care` **non-blank** | **2,541 (90.8%)** |
| — blank | 257 (9.2%) |
| — **exact zero** | **0 (0.0%)** |
| — negative | 0 |
| `Total Salaries (adjusted)` non-blank | 2,798 (100.0%) |
| **Contract labor share computable and denominator ≥ $1M** | **2,540 (90.7% of the frame)** |

### The ambiguous-zero worry does not materialise — a different problem replaces it

The plan flagged "ambiguous zero-reporting" as a hazard. **There are no zeros.** The
PUF encodes non-reporting as blank and reports only strictly positive values
(minimum $120). So the ambiguity is not zero-vs-missing; it is that a blank could
mean either "reported nothing" or "genuinely used no contract labor," and the file
gives no way to tell them apart.

Consequence for the admission gate: a blank must be **left missing, not imputed to
zero**. Imputing blanks to zero would assign 9.2% of the frame a false floor value on
the exact variable meant to measure staffing instability. The gate in
`admission-gates.md` is written accordingly.

### Distribution of the contract labor share (n = 2,540, denominator ≥ $1M)

| min | p05 | p25 | median | p75 | p95 | max |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.0000 | 0.0040 | 0.0247 | **0.0566** | 0.1034 | 0.2377 | 0.8139 |

19 hospitals exceed a 0.5 share, which is implausible as *direct patient care*
contract labor and marks either a reporting error or a genuinely outsourced staffing
model. One record is unambiguously broken (CCN 170194, `Total Salaries (adjusted)` = $1
against $4,507,434 of contract labor, giving a share of 4,507,434 — 9 beds,
210 discharges). Excluding denominators under $1M removes it and no one else.

**Verdict: the S-3 contract labor variable is admissible**, at 90.7% population, with
a plausibility ceiling. Gate written in `admission-gates.md`.

## 5. RAND Hospital Data — **substituted out**

RAND Hospital Data (`hospitaldatasets.org`) is **not fully public**. Free registration
yields a limited extract; the complete cleaned HCRIS panel requires a paid
subscription, and the current release is built on the CMS HCRIS upload of
2024-10-17 with partial-year 2023 data (6,028 hospitals, 4,595.3 hospital-years).

**Substitution:** the CMS Hospital Provider Cost Report PUF, FY 2023 Final. It is
public, complete through FY 2023, matches 99.9% of the frame, and — decisively —
already exposes the derived fields RAND's value-add would supply (uncompensated care,
occupancy inputs, margin inputs, and the S-3 contract labor line).

This substitution **removes a paid dependency and a licensing question from the
critical path**, and costs nothing measurable. Plan §4's phrase "where one exists"
anticipated this. Recorded here as a decision, not a gap.

## 6. Block C: coding and surveillance intensity — CMI level and drift, both measurable

**Level:** `TACMIV42` from the FY 2025 Impact File, 100% coverage of the matched frame.
p05 1.329, median 1.717, p95 2.537 (min 0.997, max 4.843).

**Drift:** `TACMIV42` appears in *both* the FY 2025 and FY 2026 Impact Files, computed
on FY 2023 and FY 2024 MedPAR respectively. Differencing it **holds the DRG grouper
version constant** and isolates change in coded case mix — which is exactly what a
coding-intensity proxy requires, and is not achievable by differencing `TACMIV42`
against `TACMIV43`.

Relative drift, (FY2024 − FY2023) / FY2023, computable for **2,796 of 2,801 (99.8%)**:

| min | p05 | p25 | median | p75 | p95 | p99 | max |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| −0.2539 | −0.0805 | −0.0299 | **−0.0050** | 0.0191 | 0.0680 | 0.1381 | 1.0688 |

mean −0.0046, SD 0.0527. |drift| > 10%: 122 hospitals (4.4%). |drift| > 20%: 17 (0.6%).

The distribution is tight and centred near zero, which is the expected shape and
confirms the measure is behaving. The 4.4% tail is the analytically interesting
group. The single 1.069 value is an outlier to inspect at Task 2.7, not to trim now.

**Verdict: CMI level and drift are both admissible.** Still a partial proxy — it
cannot separate more-thorough documentation from genuinely sicker patients, and plan
§4 already says so. Gate written in `admission-gates.md`.
